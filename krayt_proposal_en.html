02 — The Problem
AI deployment is broken.
Enterprises are deploying AI faster than they can secure it. Existing QA tools were built for traditional software — they miss LLM-specific attack vectors, hallucination patterns, and evolving compliance requirements.
  • No systematic LLM QA: Manual testing misses prompt injection, indirect injection, and model inversion attacks
  • Compliance blindspot: OWASP LLM Top 10, EU AI Act, Korea AI Basic Act — frameworks not integrated into dev pipelines
  • Zero reproducibility: Bug bounty findings are ad-hoc; no repeatable validation framework exists
  • Production risk: 78% of enterprise AI incidents are discovered post-deployment (Gartner, 2025)
